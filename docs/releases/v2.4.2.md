# UsageStat 2.4.2

Codex Fast and Ultrafast usage is priced correctly. UsageStat used to price every Codex request at the Standard rate, so Fast and Ultrafast sessions were shown at a half to a sixth of their API-equivalent cost. The daemon now reads the service tier from each rollout and applies OpenAI's published multiplier, including the GPT-6.2 Ultrafast launch rate (3× until March 1, 2027, then 6×). Standard sessions are unchanged. The local log cache rebuilds once on first start.

Cursor spend survives a failed dashboard query when Tokscale has a local copy of the billing events. Those days are labelled as coming from the Tokscale cache and never replace live billing. Accounts with credentials entered in UsageStat do not read the machine-wide cache.

A daemon running 2.3.1 or later restarts itself into 2.4.2 after a Linux package upgrade; Homebrew still needs `usagestat daemon relocate` after `brew upgrade`, and Windows a stop and start.
